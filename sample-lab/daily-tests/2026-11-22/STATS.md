PH: the source is 2 detuned saw voices (±3.5 c, L/R), LP 5.8k, HP 120. It runs through a 12-stage all-pass phaser with fb 0.62 (high resonance) and mix 0.5. The modulator is a RAMP-UP shape looped every 4 bars, sweeping 220 Hz plus 3.6 oct. The last 6 % of each cycle is a short cosine fall so the reset can't zipper. The R ramp is offset 0.25 cycle, so the stereo spread comes only from the phaser. After that, a mono 3/16 delay (fb 0.3) works as a filter: a narrow HP (Q 5, 450-1.5k, 0.11 Hz sine) and a narrow LP (Q 5, 1.3k-4.2k, 0.067 Hz sine) move in opposite directions, and the LFO onset is delayed by an envelope over bars 2-6. The last stage is a 100 %-wet band-limited reverb (600-2.8k, RT 3.2 s). Peak amount follows the ramp (0.25 to 1) and the reverb mix is 1 minus that, so the chain is dry at the strongest peaks and goes wet as they fade. The 4-bar swell is clearly visible as diagonal notch sweeps in the spectrogram.
RK: 4/4 kick with velocities 1/.8/.9/.8. Return A is New York compression keyed by lows <150 Hz (-30 dB thresh, 8:1, 12 ms attack, 220 ms release, which falls between a 1/16 at 156 ms and a 1/8 at 312 ms), with +4 dB low shelf at 80 Hz and +3 dB high shelf at 4 kHz. Return B is tanh drive at 15 %. Return C is a 100 %-wet small dark reverb (RT 2.6 s, size .6, damp 2.5 k), ducked by a kick-keyed compressor (250 ms release), then LP 140-520 Hz on a 1/8-synced LFO with a slow ±15 % 0.05 Hz LFO. A light tanh on the kick bus stands in for the low-end enhancer. All of it is mono.
CB: 8-bar phrasing. Intro is 4 bars (pad only, so the drums land by about 10 s). A1 has a committed turnaround at bar 7: rhythm and bass cut, with a reverb throw on the pad and lead.

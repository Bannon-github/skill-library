# volyraph-browser-splat-pipeline

**Goal:** Video to splat entirely in browser.

**Source:** Drone Video to 3D Gaussian Splat in the Browser Tutorial
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MDNBxM4FlgE

## Steps

1. Sign in to Volyraph; create and open a project.
2. Capture → Standard video; upload MP4/MOV under 5GB.
3. Wait for queue and cloud GPU processing.
4. Edit, annotate, place on map; share/export.

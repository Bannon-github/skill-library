# Organic alien comb texture: sub-audio saw click train → high-resonance filter → very short, high-feedback delay played by hand

Goal: Make an organic alien/sci-fi comb texture bed on most subtractive synths: pitch one saw all the way down until individual saw teeth are heard as a click train, run it through one low-pass at 80–100% resonance, then a delay at full mix with very short time and very long feedback (a comb resonator), adding optional comb filter, moderate pitch modulation and reverb — and perform delay time and cutoff manually (MIDI CC) rather than with modulation for an organic result. Distinct from formant/comb pad recipes that use a dedicated comb filter on sustained oscillators.

Source: [Organic Alien Comb Filter Modulation Tutorial (Subtractive Synthesis)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=acM4NM4lH0k)

1. Init the patch. Use oscillator 1 only (osc balance fully to osc 1) with a saw.
2. Turn the semitone/coarse knob all the way down until the saw becomes a click train where each tooth spike is audible.
3. Use filter 1 only; lower the cutoff a notch and raise resonance to about 80–100%.
4. Turn the delay mix all the way up, choose a very short delay time and very long feedback.
5. Play with the delay time while sounds are running to sweep the comb pitch.
6. Optional variations: add the synth's comb filter mode; add pitch modulation at a moderate speed.
7. Reduce the delay slightly and add a little reverb on top.
8. Do not modulate the filter: map cutoff (and FX parameters) to a MIDI controller via MIDI-learn and move them by hand while recording, changing timbre continuously.

# keysol-venus-high-wt-artifact-pulse-layer

Goal: Spice a classic pulsing bass/filter sequence by adding a very high-pitched wavetable oscillator mixed as an artifact-like layer, with WT-position modulation for constant subtle evolution.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLGz5wq2CRE

1. Build a classic pulsating bass sequence: pulse/saw bed, Control Track or LFO → filter cutoff, use filter feedback/saturation/drive for grit and presence.
2. Add wavetable oscillator tuned very high until it reads almost as an artifact rather than a clear tone.
3. Modulate wavetable position slowly (LFO or Control Track) for continuous micro-variation so the sequence never feels static.
4. Mix WT low under the pulse bed; A/B with WT muted to confirm it adds interest without dominating.

MCP: `skill://keysol-venus-high-wt-artifact-pulse-layer`

# keysol-venus-fm-lfo-evolving-chase-sequence

Goal: Keep long cinematic chase/arp-like sequences alive by modulating FM depth, oscillator mix via LFOs, and filter cutoff — plus mod-wheel/aftertouch for tension — instead of static one-bar loops.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLGz5wq2CRE

1. Establish rhythmic pulse/sequence foundation (arp or control-track → cutoff). Aim for cinematic intensity rather than clean EDM purity.
2. Introduce FM: vary modulator frequency slightly and automate/modulate FM depth so timbre breathes over the phrase.
3. Use LFOs to change oscillator mix balances over time; also modulate filter cutoff so the sequence refuses to feel static across long cues.
4. Assign mod wheel / aftertouch to open tension (depth, cutoff, or drive) for scene dynamics.
5. Play deeper registers for more body when the cue needs weight; keep FX (delay bounce) feeding syncopated rhythms for living grooves.

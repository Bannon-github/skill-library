# keysol-venus-noise-audiorate-filter-texture

Goal: Create a radioactive/unstable cinematic texture or lead: dual detuned saws + WT character, aftertouch bringing in a sharp high oscillator, and mod-wheel sending noise (~30% mix) into audio-rate-style filter modulation (plus optional Osc2→cutoff always-on grit).

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLGz5wq2CRE

1. Layer two fairly separate saw oscillators plus a wavetable osc for character. Slightly detune one osc; further detune can yield ring-mod-like artifacts.
2. Optionally enable continuous Osc2 → low-pass cutoff (audio-rate-ish FM-of-filter) for granular grit; A/B it to hear the instability.
3. Mix noise around ~30%. Map mod wheel to send noise into LPF cutoff modulation — annihilating cutoff with noise yields extremely unstable/distorted color.
4. Use aftertouch (or pressure) to bring in a high-frequency oscillator for resonant overtones that feel like distortion byproducts when played low.
5. Play as lead, texture, or pre-scare sting; leave headroom — this patch is intentionally unstable.

MCP: `skill://keysol-venus-noise-audiorate-filter-texture`

# Nested multi-notch phaser techniques for pads: grouped notches (groups × notches-per-group), pinch spacing, slow pinch 'breathing', stepped cutoff wah→flanger, scale-quantized random cutoff, slow triangle-swept full-depth bed

Goal: Use a dense, nested phaser (notches arranged in up to ~6 groups × ~11 notches, one cutoff sliding all of them, resonance = notch width, depth = intensity, pinch = squeeze/spread within each group) as a cinematic pad mover: metallic shimmer layer (4 groups, 9 notches, depth ~82, raise pinch), inside-out 'tubular' breathing (pinch modulated by a slow quarter-note-synced LFO, depth ~63, try 2 vs 4 groups), static 'radio' sculpt (1 group, 1 notch, depth ~72), 1/16 stepped cutoff that turns from wah (1 notch) into flanger-like (7–9 notches) with glide, formant-like vowel push by slowly lowering cutoff (4/7, depth 65, low resonance), scale-quantized random cutoff (1/16, minor pentatonic, ~7 octave range) that stays in tune, and a slow breathing bed (6 groups, 11 notches, depth 100%, triangle sweep with ~10% glide).

Source: [You’ve Never Heard a Phaser Sound Like This](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H8fsiSJzKTA)

1. Map the controls: groups ('nests'), notches per group ('peaks'), cutoff (moves all notches), resonance (notch width), depth (0 transparent → full phaser), pinch (squeeze/spread notches within each group).
2. Shimmer layer on a pad: 4 groups, 9 notches, depth ~82; slowly raise pinch until a metallic bell-like shimmer appears without taking over.
3. Tubular breathing: same density (4/9), depth ~63; modulate pinch with a slow LFO synced to ~1/4 note. Compare 4 groups vs 2 groups — same motion, different character.
4. Static sculpt: 1 group, 1 notch, depth ~72 for a radio/telephone colour; move the notch up for 'pickup switch' tones; add groups to smooth it.
5. Stepped wah→flanger: 1/16-synced sequencer on cutoff at ~40–50% amount, 1 notch (wah); raise to 7–9 notches for a flanger-like texture; add glide so steps don't snap.
6. Vowel push on vocal-like material: 4 groups, 7 notches, depth 65, resonance low (~4), slowly lower cutoff.
7. Tuned random: random modulator on cutoff at ~1/16, quantized to a scale (e.g. minor pentatonic), range ~7.2 octaves. Change the scale to change the mood.
8. Breathing bed under a pad: 6 groups, 11 notches (maximum density), depth 100%, cutoff swept by a slow triangle sequence (rate ~2) with ~10% glide; then vary pattern, pinch and depth.

# keysol-venus-polyrhythmic-control-track-pitch

Goal: Build a never-quite-repeating cinematic sequence by driving Osc1/Osc2 pitch from two Control Tracks with irregular lengths (e.g. 15 and 14) and independent rates (e.g. dotted quarter vs quarter), over a stable low Osc3 pulse bed.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLGz5wq2CRE

1. Create a stable bed on Osc3 (e.g. pulse) an octave or two below; add delay/space lightly so the bed feels cinematic but steady.
2. Raise Osc1 and Osc2 to much higher pitch registers; they need not sound pretty alone — they supply character over the bed.
3. Control Track A → Osc1 pitch, length 15, rate dotted-quarter (or similar). Control Track B → Osc2 pitch, length 14, rate quarter. Keep modulation depth small so pitch breaks subtly rather than jumping wildly.
4. Map mod wheel (or expression) to increase the depth of those pitch mods for performance tension.
5. Mix: keep Osc3 dominant; blend high oscs underneath. Solo high pair to verify the irregular interlocking, then restore full mix.

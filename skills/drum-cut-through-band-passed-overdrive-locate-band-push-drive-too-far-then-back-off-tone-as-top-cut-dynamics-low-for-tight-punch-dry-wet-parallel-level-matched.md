# Make a weak drum (e.g. a clap) cut through and drive the groove with band-passed overdrive: choose the band that gets distorted, push the drive too far then back off, use tone to tame harsh top, set the dynamics control low for a tight punch, blend dry/wet, and level-match

Goal: Fix a drum hit that sounds wimpy or sits on top of the mix. On the soloed hit (reverb off, low cut kept so it doesn't fight the kick and bass), insert an overdrive with a band-pass before the distortion stage, inside a group with a gain utility after it for level matching. Sweep the band-pass to find the range you want to emphasize (avoid the thick, murky lows) and narrow the Q roughly. Push the drive hard to hear what it does, then back it off (about 60%) so the brittle upper-mid hiss doesn't dominate. Use the tone control as a top-end cut. Set the dynamics control lower for a tighter, punchier, shorter-feeling hit, or higher for a washed-out, relaxed one (it acts like note length and articulation for the groove). Dial dry/wet (parallel) from about 50 to 100%. Level-match against bypass with the gain utility, then put the reverb back and fine-tune in context, leaving final tweaks for the mixdown.

5. Set the dynamics control low (about 20-30) for tight punch, or higher for a looser, washed-out feel.

# drone-hyperlapse-multi-angle-capture

**Goal:** Capture overlapping views with DJI hyperlapse.

**Source:** How to Do Drone Gaussian Splatting: Beginner Tutorial (Splatware)
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FpN2VTSZ_7U

## Steps

1. Plan multi-angle coverage of every facade (photogrammetry-style).
2. Use DJI built-in hyperlapse modes.
3. Fly slowly; avoid blur; export media.

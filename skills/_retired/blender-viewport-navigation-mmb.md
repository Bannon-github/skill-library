# blender viewport navigation with middle mouse

Goal: Orbit, pan, zoom, and snap orthographic views in Blender's 3D viewport.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=peSv5IT5Ve4

1. Scroll the middle mouse wheel to zoom in and out.
2. Click and drag the middle mouse button to orbit around the object.
3. Hold Shift and middle-mouse-drag to pan the scene.
4. If you have a numpad: 1 front orthographic, 3 right, 7 top, 9 bottom.
5. Without a numpad: hold Alt, middle-mouse-drag toward the desired axis to snap orthographic views.

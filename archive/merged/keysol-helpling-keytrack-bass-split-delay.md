# keysol-helpling-keytrack-bass-split-delay

Goal: Make a simple expressive patch where delay can dominate the mix, and a low oscillator’s level is key-tracked so bass appears mainly in the left-hand/low register while upper notes stay clearer.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJWS77wyVe0

1. Create a simple two-layer patch: mid/high tonal oscillator(s) plus a dedicated low oscillator.
2. Modulate the low oscillator’s volume with keyboard tracking so it is strong only in the lower register (left hand / low keys) and fades as you play higher.
3. Set delay wet very high (delay almost louder than the dry sound) to showcase modulation/space; filter delay feedback to taste.

MCP: `skill://keysol-helpling-keytrack-bass-split-delay`

# Design four bass types from scratch with fit-to-kick rules: a punchy 808 (sine, hold and 1.5 s decay, a 35 ms pitch-envelope punch, tube drive, low boost, attack delayed 80-100 ms when the kick already punches), a pluck bass (mono square through an enveloped 24 dB low-pass plus a wide unison top layer), a sidechained saw growl, and a click-free sine sub

Goal: Four punchy bass recipes. 808: one sine osc, attack near 0 (raise slightly until the click disappears), hold about 300-400 ms, decay about 1.5 s, sustain 0; a little wavetable bend/warp for character; a second envelope (attack about 0.5 ms, hold 0, decay about 35 ms, sustain 0, release about 15 ms) routed to master tune for a kick-like pitch punch (set amount to the middle); tube distortion with mix pulled back; EQ lift around 100 Hz. If the kick already supplies the punch, raise the 808 attack to about 80-100 ms so it fills in behind the kick instead of sidechaining. Pluck/house bass: mono voice, square wave through a 24 dB low-pass, short attack, no hold, low sustain, some release; the same envelope also drives cutoff for the pluck; drive the filter a little; add a second osc (PWM-type wave, 5-7 unison voices, detuned) for a wide top, keep content below about 100-150 Hz mono, reverb and delay with low cut. Growl bass: saw with a low-pass, second osc with 7-8 unison voices, mono, very short release, an LFO shaped as a sidechain duck on both osc levels, cutoff on a macro, then boost about 140 Hz to keep it a bass. Sub: sine, one voice, mono/legato, attack about 5 ms and a short release to avoid clicks.

1. 808 body: sine osc, attack near 0 then raised just enough to remove the click, hold about 300-400 ms, decay about 1.5 s, sustain 0; add a slight bend/warp to the wave for character.
2. 808 punch: second envelope (attack about 0.5 ms, hold 0, decay about 35 ms, sustain 0, release about 15 ms) → master/global tune; set the amount between too little and too much.
3. 808 FX: tube distortion with mix pulled back, EQ lift around 100 Hz. Play with the drums: if both kick and 808 punch, raise the 808 attack to about 80-100 ms so the kick provides the transient.
4. Pluck bass: mono voicing, square osc into a 24 dB low-pass; amp envelope with tiny attack, no hold, low sustain, a bit of release; route the same envelope to cutoff and add a little filter drive.
5. Pluck top layer: second osc with a PWM-style wave, 5-7 unison voices, detune set for width, through the same filter at lower level; add reverb and delay with their lows cut; keep lows mono below about 100-150 Hz.
6. Growl bass: saw + low-pass, second saw osc with 7-8 unison voices, mono, very short release; draw an LFO shaped like a sidechain duck and route it to both osc levels; put cutoff (and optionally reverb/delay mix) on macros; boost around 140 Hz.
7. Sub: sine, 1 voice, mono/legato, attack about 5 ms, short-but-not-instant release; play low.

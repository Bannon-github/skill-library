# Brooding cinematic drone: three pulse-width-morph oscillators, opposed octave detune, single-osc vibrato, LFO PW breathing and split pitch-bend depths

Goal: Build a massive, brooding cinematic drone in any capable synth: three pulse-width-morphing square oscillators (two an octave down, one wide 4-voice unison), the two low oscillators nudged slightly sharp and flat around the octave for clash, vibrato on only one oscillator, a slow LFO breathing one oscillator's pulse-width morph, a 12 dB low-pass on the mod wheel, reverb plus filtered delay, and different extra pitch-bend depths per oscillator so bends spread instead of moving in lockstep.

Source: [How to create Massive, Brooding, Cinematic Drones in any Synth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DFLshMLKkHE)

1. Use three oscillators, all set to a pulse-width-shifting square (a square whose pulse width can morph). Square/odd-harmonic content gives the hollow, brooding foundation.
2. Osc 1: move the shape slightly away from pure square for a biting edge, use 2 voices slightly detuned, add a slight vibrato, tune -12 semitones.
3. Osc 2: keep a square, tune -12 semitones, 4 unison voices detuned very wide.
4. Osc 3: leave at the higher (unshifted) pitch, give it a different pulse shape, 2 voices slightly detuned.
5. Amp envelope 'pad treatment': slow attack so the sound creeps in, long tapering release.
6. Add drama/clash: nudge the two octave-down oscillators by a small fraction in opposite directions (presenter's display: about -12.10 and -11.90) and offset Osc 3 slightly (~.5 in the synth's fine units).
7. Restrict vibrato to one oscillator only; the others get none.
8. Set pitch-bend range to +-1 so you can bend up and down by small amounts.
9. Assign a slow LFO to Osc 3's waveform/pulse-width morph for a little breathing.
10. Add a 12 dB low-pass (cascade-style) to tame harshness and map the mod wheel to cutoff; start notes with the mod wheel up and ride it.
11. Global FX: reverb with larger size and more wet so it holds on; then a delay (~140 in the presenter's units) with a little feedback and high-pass/low-pass on the repeats.
12. Make bends spread: add a small extra pitch-wheel -> tune amount on Osc 1 (~20) and a larger one on Osc 3 (tried ~75, settled near 60 and then pulled slightly lower) so oscillators bend at slightly different rates.

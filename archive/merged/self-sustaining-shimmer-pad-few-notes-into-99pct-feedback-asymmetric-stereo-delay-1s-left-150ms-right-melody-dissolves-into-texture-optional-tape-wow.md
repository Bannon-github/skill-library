# Turn a few played notes into a self-sustaining shimmering pad by feeding them once into a clean stereo delay at 99% feedback with very different left/right times (about 1 s and 150 ms), so the melody dissolves into a continuous texture for the rest of the track

Goal: Feedback-delay pad generator. Play a short phrase of three or four notes once into a neutral (uncoloured) stereo delay; nothing else is fed in for the rest of the track. Set feedback to the maximum safe value (99%) and set the delay times far apart: about 1 s on the left and about 150 ms on the right. The repeats keep cycling through the whole track; the original melody vanishes and is replaced by a shimmering secondary texture. Optional: use a tape-style delay instead for pitch warble, which strengthens the shimmer. Use the plain feedback control rather than cross-feed, which grows the signal quickly.

Source: [How to Create a Shimmering Pad](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r45X4PYH-fg)

1. Write a 3-4 note phrase and route it to a stereo delay set 100% wet on that channel (or on a send).
2. Set left delay about 1 s and right delay about 150 ms; set feedback to 99% on both sides; leave cross-feed at zero.
3. Play the phrase once and let it run; do not feed more notes in.
4. Optional: swap to a tape-style delay with light wow/flutter to exaggerate shimmer.
5. Add a limiter or level automation after the delay and check the level over the full track length.

# Three-pillar cinematic drone (layering, movement, FX): wavetable VCO pair + self-feedback delay loop + granular layer, effects after filters with master low-cut as tension control

Goal: Build a cinematic drone from three layers and three pillars: (1) a wavetable VCO pair (second one octave down on a slowly volume-modulated VCA) through a low-pass with slow LFO on table position; (2) a self-oscillating feedback loop from a granular/delay processor with no input (feedback raised until it sings, any re-patched input attenuated) through its own resonant low-pass; (3) a granular layer recording 2–3 random sources, with intensity, short grain length for lo-fi, quantized pitch randomization, follow/auto-scan, overdub, S&H choosing layers and an LFO on grain length — all summed through a master filter BEFORE a shimmer reverb so resonance in the low band excites the reverb, using the filter's low cut/cutoff as the scene's tension control (more highs = tension, more lows = suspense).

Source: [Ep. 04 | How to Build Powerful Cinematic Drone Sounds (Modular Synth Sound Design)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aNVjrDQANMw)

1. Layer 1: two wavetable VCOs on separate VCA channels, VCO2 one octave below; slowly modulate VCO2's level so the sub appears sometimes; low-pass to cut buzzy harmonics.
2. Movement on layer 1: very slow LFO on wavetable position/axis; optionally resonance/drive/wave-shaper for more harmonics.
3. Layer 2 (feedback loop): with no input, raise the processor's feedback until it starts self-generating; keep delay lines close; run it into its own resonant low-pass; if feeding output back to input, attenuate it first and lower input gain.
4. Layer 3 (granular): record 2–3 different short sources (even a click or mic sound) as layers; set intensity (grain count), short grain length for lo-fi, quantized pitch randomization; use follow mode to auto-scan through the recording, forward or reverse; overdub (e.g. whistling) for new tones.
5. Movement on layer 3: sample-and-hold selects which recorded layer plays; an LFO modulates grain length.
6. Sum layers, then a master low-pass with resonance BEFORE shimmer reverb and delay so resonant low harmonics feed the reverb.
7. Use the master filter's low cut / cutoff as the tension control: more high frequencies = tension ('something is coming'), lows = suspense; choose reverb type for mood; end a scene by pulling inputs out and leaving a little wet.

# keysol-venus-velocity-hybrid-bass-impact

Goal: Design a thunderous hybrid bass/impact that responds exponentially to velocity (louder hits open filter/amp dramatically), usable as deep hits or high atmospheric stabs with HP + long space reverb.

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jLGz5wq2CRE

1. Start with dark hybrid oscillators (saw/pulse + optional WT). Fast amp attack, punchy decay, moderate sustain for bass; shorter for impacts.
2. Map velocity strongly (near-exponential) to filter cutoff and/or drive so louder playing blooms dramatically.
3. For atmospheric use: apply aggressive high-pass on the mix, play high register with pitch bend, and use a broad smooth space-style reverb so hits become huge pads/stingers.
4. For deep bass: keep HP low/off, emphasize transient, optionally underscoring with sub; avoid static sine-only subs — keep some transient character.

MCP: `skill://keysol-venus-velocity-hybrid-bass-impact`

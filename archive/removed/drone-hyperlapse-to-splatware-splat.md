# Drone hyperlapse capture to Gaussian splat in free Splatware

Goal: Capture overlapping multi-angle views of a building/subject with DJI built-in hyperlapse modes (photogrammetry-style coverage of every facade, slow flight to avoid blur, no paid flight planner), then upload the hyperlapse/video to the free Splatware web app, process, review, and download the Gaussian splat.

Sources (merged 2026-10-03):
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FpN2VTSZ_7U (from `drone-hyperlapse-multi-angle-capture`)
- https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FpN2VTSZ_7U (from `upload-capture-to-free-splatware`)

1. Plan multi-angle coverage of every facade (photogrammetry-style).
2. Use DJI built-in hyperlapse modes.
3. Fly slowly; avoid blur; export media.
4. Open free Splatware web app.
5. Upload hyperlapse/video.
6. Run processing; review and download the splat.
